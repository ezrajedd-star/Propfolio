# Playbook: handing over well

A hand-over (`ticket_handover`) is read by the founder in a hurry. Make it complete:

- **summary**: one or two sentences: who (plan, how far set up), what they want.
- **tried**: what you looked up or suggested, if anything.
- **reason**: the rule that applies ("refund request", "customer asked for a person", "unsure:
  sounds like a bug in renewals but I can't tell").
- **nextStep**: what a person should do first ("check the October payment in Xendit", "call
  them; they sounded upset").

Then stop: don't also draft a reply, unless a short holding reply helps ("Thanks — a person
from our team will reply today."), and then set the matching flag so it waits for approval.

Hand over when the customer asks for a person, at once, without trying to solve it first.
