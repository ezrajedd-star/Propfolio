# Playbook: where things are in Propfolio

Use these names exactly, so customers can follow along.

- **Home**: today's reminders, leases ending soon, rent due, the Getting started list.
- **Calendar**: lease ends, renewals and rent due dates by month.
- **Leases → New lease**: pick the unit and tenant, set dates and rent; **Renew** and **End
  early** are on each lease's page.
- **Units**: occupancy; buildings and units are added in **Settings → Buildings and units**
  (User Admins), or many at once with **Add several units**.
- **Tenants → Add tenant**: the privacy notice must be acknowledged first.
- **Payments**: record a payment against a lease's charges; **Void** with a reason (payments
  are never deleted).
- **Reports**: rent roll, collections, aging; **Export** to Excel.
- **Settings** (Owner and User Admins): Users, General, Buildings and units, Unit types and
  reminders, Reminder emails, Privacy, Import from Excel, Audit log, Propfolio support access,
  Your data (Owner).
- **Plan**: Free (3 units, 1 person), Pro (₱1,500 a month, 50 units, 1 person), Premium
  (₱3,500 a month, 200 units, 5 people). New accounts get 30 days of Premium free.
- **My account**: name, password, two-step sign-in. Owners and User Admins must use two-step
  sign-in.
- **Help → My requests**: their tickets and our replies.

Roles: Owner, User Admin, Leasing Staff, Property Management (read-only, sees tenant names only).
