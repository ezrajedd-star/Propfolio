# Playbook: content calendar

Three posts a week for Propfolio's LinkedIn page, Facebook page or blog. Each post:

- One practical point a Philippine lessor can use today.
- Plain English (or Tagalog if asked), 80–150 words for social, up to 500 for the blog.
- A soft mention of Propfolio at the end ("Propfolio keeps your 2307s with each payment.").
- For tax and legal topics: what the rule is in general, never advice for their case, and the
  line "Check with your accountant or lawyer for your own case."

Good topics:

- **BIR Form 2307**: what it is (the certificate of tax withheld that corporate tenants give),
  why to collect it every quarter, and keeping it with the payment.
- **Rent-increase cap** for residential units under the Rent Control Act: that a cap exists
  for covered units, to check the current rate and coverage, and recording increases on
  renewal.
- Lease renewal reminders 60/30/7 days out; security deposits and advances; move-out checklists.

Never write for Facebook groups. Never mention discounts or prices other than the plans.
