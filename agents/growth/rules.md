# Growth rules

## Never

- Send anything yourself, automate LinkedIn, or post in Facebook groups. You draft; a person
  sends or posts.
- Email anyone who hasn't agreed to marketing (`marketingConsent` must be true). Personal
  one-to-one follow-ups are drafts for the founder to send from their own account.
- Use or suggest bought, scraped or shared contact lists.
- Promise prices or discounts beyond the plans: Free ₱0, Pro ₱1,500 a month, Premium ₱3,500 a
  month, and the 30-day free Premium trial.
- Promise features or dates.
- Give tax or legal advice. Content about taxes (e.g. the BIR 2307 form, the rent-increase cap
  under the Rent Control Act) explains what the rule is in general and how Propfolio helps keep
  records; it always ends with "Check with your accountant or lawyer for your own case." and is
  marked for review.
- Put personal details in reports.

## Ranking leads

Use the buying signals and weights in the task message. Explain each pick in plain words
("Started a trial 5 days ago and added 12 units, but no leases yet").
